Case study · SES-MG · Public health

Following every child from screening to glasses

A state program that screens public school students for vision and hearing problems, and makes sure each one gets care.

Client
Minas Gerais State Health Department (SES-MG)
My role
Product Designer
Platform
Web
Validation
With the Department’s team, as they used it
The problem

No way to know where each child was

Before the system there was no real control. Nothing showed which children had been screened, referred, seen by a doctor, or had already received their glasses or hearing aids.

A child could easily get lost between one step and the next.

  1. 01Screening
  2. 02Referral
  3. 03Appointment
  4. 04Glasses delivered
Key decisions

What I designed

01

Funnel cards at the top of every step

Totals and what is still pending: screenings with issues, referrals, appointments and glasses delivered. Whoever opens any screen sees right away where the program is stuck.

my idea
02

Bulk actions, with a review step

Teams refer many students at once. Before confirming, they check the selected list in a review window, so nobody is referred by mistake.

03

One record per student

Student, school, screening, eye exam and deliveries on a single page, ready to print. Anyone picking up a case has the whole history in one place.

04

Indicators for the whole program

A dashboard for vision and hearing, filtered by region and municipality, shows how many children moved through each step over the years.

Outcome

  • The Department’s team used the system as it was being built and gave continuous feedback, which shaped each iteration.
  • Every child now has a traceable path, from screening to delivery, visible to everyone involved.
